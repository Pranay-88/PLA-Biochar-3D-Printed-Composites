# PLA-Biochar 3D Printed Composites

## Overview

This repository presents my published research on the fabrication and characterization of Biochar reinforced PLA composite filament for FDM 3D printing.

The work investigates how different biochar contents affect:

- Tensile Strength
- Flexural Strength
- Wear Resistance
- Impact Strength
- Thermal Conductivity
- Surface Morphology (SEM)

---

## Publication

Published in Springer.

Title:

**Fabrication and Comprehensive Characterization of Enhanced Biochar–PLA 3D Printed Composite Filament**
